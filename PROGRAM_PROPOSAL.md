# Program proposal (awaiting approval)

Free weights first: Ryan and Stella train together, so a spotter is present. The Smith bar is used where it is the better tool and as the "training alone today" swap. Nothing here is seeded until approved.

Equipment assumed: RitFit M1 Pro (Smith bar 33 lb, two cables at 2:1, lat bar, short bar, D-handles, rope, V-handle, close-grip row handle, landmine, dip handles, pull-up bar, J-hooks, safety arms), 45 lb barbell, adjustable bench, dumbbell pairs 5 to 50 lb in 5 lb steps, bumper plates (pairs of 10, 15, 25, 35, 45), change plates (pairs of 1.25, 2.5, 5), one ankle strap.

## Main lifts

Main lifts stay the same for at least 8 to 12 weeks. Only the rep range changes with the phase.

| Day | Main lift 1 | Main lift 2 | "Training alone" swaps | Other alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Legs & Lower Back | Barbell back squat | Barbell Romanian deadlift | Smith squat; dumbbell Romanian deadlift | Goblet squat, landmine squat; Smith Romanian deadlift |
| B Arms, Upper Back & Shoulders | Barbell bench press | Ryan: pull-up, adding weight over time. Stella: lat pulldown, plus pull-up practice | Smith bench press; no swap needed | Dumbbell bench press; chin-up, neutral-grip pulldown |
| C Core & Cardio | Hanging leg raise (knees bent to start) | Barbell rollout (from the knees) | None needed | Lying leg raise; plank walkout, long-lever plank |
| D Full Body | Barbell deadlift (from the floor, on bumpers) | Barbell overhead press (standing) | None needed; Smith overhead press | Trap-style dumbbell deadlift; seated dumbbell press, landmine press |

Across the week this covers: squat, hinge twice (one lighter, one heavy), horizontal press, vertical press, vertical pull. Horizontal pulling (rows) is the first accessory slot on Day B and a standing slot on Day D. Incline pressing is a fixed slot on Day B in every block.

The two programs are separate, so the same slot can hold a different lift for each person. Day B's pull is the first case: Ryan can do 10 to 20 pull-ups, so he trains them as a main lift and adds weight (a dumbbell held between the feet) once he reaches 12 on every set. Stella can do 1 to 2, which is too few to train in sets, so her main lift is the lat pulldown and she practises pull-ups first: a few single reps and slow lowerings. She moves to pull-ups as her main lift once she can do 5.

Rep ranges by phase:

| Phase | Squat, bench, overhead press, pulldown, Romanian deadlift | Deadlift | Accessories |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 8 to 12 | 6 to 8 | 10 to 15 |
| Build | 8 to 10 | 5 to 8 | 10 to 15 |
| Strength | 5 to 8 | 3 to 6 | 8 to 12 |

The deadlift runs lower reps in every phase: high-rep sets from the floor tire the lower back before the legs and hips get trained.

## Gap analysis and accessory slots

Each slot is a role. The exercise filling it rotates at the start of each 4-week block. Slots are listed in priority order; a session with 3 accessories uses slots 1 to 3, a session with 7 uses all of them.

Both main lifts scale by person. The hanging leg raise starts with bent knees and progresses to straight legs, then toes toward the bar. The barbell rollout starts from the knees over a short range and lengthens as it gets easier; it is the hardest lift on this day, so stop a set as soon as the lower back starts to sag.

Then the cardio block: type, duration, distance, optional steps.

## First block (Foundation, 4 weeks)

- Main lifts: 3 sets. Accessories: 2 sets.
- Accessory count per session: week 1 has 4, week 2 has 5, week 3 has 6, week 4 (recovery) has 3.
- Main lifts stop with about 2 reps left; accessories with 1 to 2.
- Session 1 of each day is a guided weight-finding session: start light and add weight each set until the target reps feel like 2 are left.
- Small accessories are paired as supersets once a session has 5 or more, to stay within 45 to 70 minutes.

## Known limits

- One ankle strap means cable leg curls, kickbacks and hip work are done one leg at a time, which is how they are normally done anyway.
- Day B asks a lot of grip (pull-ups, rows, then the grip slot). The grip slot is last for that reason and is the first to be dropped on a short session.
- Squat safety: confirm the safety arms catch the bar at the bottom of your squat before loading up. One review says they are short.
- One barbell: Day B uses it for bench, rows and curls in sequence. The engine orders the day to keep plate changes small.
- These set and rep numbers come from general training practice, not from a study lookup, and can be tuned.
