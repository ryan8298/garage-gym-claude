# Program proposal (awaiting approval)

Free weights first: Ryan and Stella train together, so a spotter is present. The Smith bar is used where it is the better tool and as the "training alone today" swap. Nothing here is seeded until approved.

Equipment assumed: RitFit M1 Pro (Smith bar 33 lb, two cables at 2:1, lat bar, short bar, D-handles, rope, V-handle, close-grip row handle, landmine, dip handles, pull-up bar, J-hooks, safety arms), 45 lb barbell, adjustable bench, dumbbell pairs 5 to 50 lb in 5 lb steps, bumper plates (pairs of 10, 15, 25, 35, 45), change plates (pairs of 1.25, 2.5, 5).

## Main lifts

Main lifts stay the same for at least 8 to 12 weeks. Only the rep range changes with the phase.

| Day | Main lift 1 | Main lift 2 | "Training alone" swaps | Other alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Legs & Lower Back | Barbell back squat | Barbell Romanian deadlift | Smith squat; dumbbell Romanian deadlift | Goblet squat, landmine squat; Smith Romanian deadlift |
| B Arms, Upper Back & Shoulders | Barbell bench press | Lat pulldown (toward pull-ups) | Smith bench press; no swap needed | Dumbbell bench press; assisted pull-up, chin-up |
| C Core & Cardio | Cable crunch (rope) | Farmer carry | None needed | Hanging knee raise; suitcase carry, Pallof press |
| D Full Body | Barbell deadlift (from the floor, on bumpers) | Barbell overhead press (standing) | None needed; Smith overhead press | Trap-style dumbbell deadlift; seated dumbbell press, landmine press |

Across the week this covers: squat, hinge twice (one lighter, one heavy), horizontal press, vertical press, vertical pull. Horizontal pulling (rows) is the first accessory slot on Day B and a standing slot on Day D.

Rep ranges by phase:

| Phase | Squat, bench, overhead press, pulldown, Romanian deadlift | Deadlift | Accessories |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 8 to 12 | 6 to 8 | 10 to 15 |
| Build | 8 to 10 | 5 to 8 | 10 to 15 |
| Strength | 5 to 8 | 3 to 6 | 8 to 12 |

The deadlift runs lower reps in every phase: high-rep sets from the floor tire the lower back before the legs and hips get trained.

## First block (Foundation, 4 weeks)

- Main lifts: 3 sets. Accessories: 2 sets.
- Accessory count per session: week 1 has 4, week 2 has 5, week 3 has 6, week 4 (recovery) has 3.
- Main lifts stop with about 2 reps left; accessories with 1 to 2.
- Session 1 of each day is a guided weight-finding session: start light and add weight each set until the target reps feel like 2 are left.
- Small accessories are paired as supersets once a session has 5 or more, to stay within 45 to 70 minutes.

## Known limits

- Hamstring curls are the weakest slot. There is no leg curl machine, so the pool relies on a dumbbell held between the feet and partner Nordics. An ankle strap (about $10) would add cable leg curls and kickbacks.
- Squat safety: confirm the safety arms catch the bar at the bottom of your squat before loading up. One review says they are short.
- One barbell: Day B uses it for bench, rows and curls in sequence. The engine orders the day to keep plate changes small.
- These set and rep numbers come from general training practice, not from a study lookup, and can be tuned.
